    Pilsbot

    Pilsbot is a hobby sidewalk robot on a hoverboard drivetrain with a passively steered front axle. This glossary covers the whole workspace, including the robot, its software lines, and its recorded data.

    Vehicle

    Front axle: The steerable axle that carries the two driven hoverboard wheels and pivots relative to the chassis. Avoid: steering axle (when meaning the whole axle), drive axle

    Rear axle: The rigid, unpowered axle fixed to the chassis.

    Rear axle centre: The midpoint of the rear axle, which is the robot's reference point for pose, odometry, and planning. Avoid: base (unqualified), robot origin

    Wheelbase: The distance from the front axle's pivot to the rear axle centre.

    Steering angle: The angle between the front axle and the chassis, measured at the pivot. Avoid: steering position, pot value (for the calibrated angle)

    Passive steering: Turning the front axle by driving its two wheels at different speeds, since no motor acts on the pivot.

    Acker-diff steering: Pilsbot's drive scheme, which accepts Ackermann commands (speed and steering angle) and realises them through passive steering. Avoid: diff drive, Ackermann drive (alone)

    Endstop: A switch marking the mechanical limit of the steering angle on either side.

    Hoverboard: The repurposed hoverboard controller board and its two hub-motor wheels, which together drive the front axle.

    Head MCU: The microcontroller on the front axle that reads the steering potentiometer and the endstops.

    Robot profile: The single description of one physical Pilsbot: geometry, steering calibration, device ports, and joystick mapping. Avoid: config, robot yaml

    StVO lighting: Headlight, turn signal, brake, and hazard behaviour that follows German road traffic rules (StVO). Avoid: indicators (for the whole set), LEDs

    Perception

    OAK: The OAK-D Lite camera, which provides colour, stereo depth, and on-camera neural inference. Avoid: depthai (for the device), Luxonis camera

    Drivability map: A robot-local map that labels the ground ahead as drivable sidewalk, road, or not drivable. Avoid: costmap (for the semantic layer), segmentation map

    Software lines

    Humble line: The primary development line of all Pilsbot repositories, targeting ROS 2 Humble.

    Foxy line: The maintained legacy line targeting ROS 2 Foxy, which receives backports of substantial distro-independent improvements. Avoid: old branch, foxy-devel

    Camera container: The Nano container with Jetson L4T libraries that owns the OAK and its hardware-accelerated decoding.

    Stack container: The Nano container that runs everything else from Humble binary packages.

    Data

    Kohlfahrt log: A bag recorded during a real outing with the robot, used as raw material for datasets.

    Legacy log: A bag recorded before the robot reference point moved to the rear axle centre, which must be translated before it is used with current frames.
